## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Heroes & Dice** is an engaging action game available on Windows that combines the excitement of dynamic dice rolling with strategic gameplay. Players embody unique heroes, each with their own dice, to determine their fate in thrilling battles. The **latest version** of Heroes & Dice features full functionality, allowing players to reroll selected dice to develop their strategies while battling various enemies. As players progress through the game, they accumulate coins to unlock new skills, enhancing character customization and development.
